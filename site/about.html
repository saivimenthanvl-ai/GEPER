About GEPER

GEPER is a biotechnology and artificial intelligence company building AI for genomics.

What GEPER does

GEPER operates at the intersection of biotechnology, artificial intelligence, and genomics. We develop computational technologies designed to work with complex biological and genomic data, with a focus on building practical AI systems for genomic research and analysis.

Mission

To build intelligent technologies that help transform complex genomic data into useful biological insight.

Founder

Founded by Kislay Prasad, to build biotechnology and AI systems focused on genomics and computational biology.

“GEPER began with the belief that the next generation of biological discovery will be built where computation and biology meet.”

— Kislay Prasad, Founder, GEPER
